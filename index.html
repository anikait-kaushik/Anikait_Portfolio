01 Research

Understanding the dynamics of how humans and robots move, then building machines around it.

I'm interested in robots that move through, and work alongside, the physical world: legged and wearable systems, manipulators and field robots. My research so far combines Euler–Lagrange modelling with instrumented experiments, and checks every model against measured data.

Research interests with current evidence for each

Legged locomotion

How legs generate, absorb and redistribute energy, and how models of human gait can inform legged and wearable robots.

Evidence: three-link gait dynamics model (T-RO, under review) · hands-on commissioning of a Unitree Go2 Pro EDU

Robot dynamics & modelling

Euler–Lagrange formulations that are compact enough to use and accurate enough to trust, validated against measurement.

Evidence: model validated against Visual3D across five subjects · Dynamics of Robotic Systems (AIR 2025, IIT Jodhpur)

Exoskeletons & physical HRI

Wearable lower-limb exoskeletons for rehabilitation, where robot motion has to be judged by human biomechanics.

Evidence: R3 programme, TIET–UQ Centre of Excellence (ongoing since Jun 2025)

Manipulation

Manipulator design and integration for field tasks: reaching, servicing hardware and handling samples.

Evidence: Ganga's 5-DOF manipulator (team captain) · Orangewood arm pick-and-place · LoCoBot Sim2Real (ongoing)

Control & Sim2Real

Using physics-based digital twins to develop behaviour in simulation that holds up on hardware.

Evidence: MATLAB–ROS framework with a Simscape LoCoBot twin, with MathWorks (in progress)

Autonomous field robotics

Robots that complete mission tasks in unstructured terrain: mobility, sampling, instrument deployment and autonomy.

Evidence: Ganga (IRC 2026, 1st/33) · Alaknanda (IRC 2025, 2nd/31)

R1 · Gait dynamics · TIET–UQ R3Under review · IEEE T-RO

A unified Euler–Lagrange framework for human gait, using a three-link leg model

Aim: test whether a compact three-link (thigh–shank–foot) Euler–Lagrange model, driven by measured motion and force data, agrees with an established biomechanics pipeline.

1 · ModelThree-link sagittal leg; equations of motion via Euler–Lagrange
2 · CaptureQualisys motion capture, AMTI force plates, Noraxon EMG
3 · ProcessSynchronised in QTM; 3D reconstruction; filtering & analysis in MATLAB
4 · ValidateCompared against Visual3D across five subjects
My role and what's still open

I developed the experimental workflow in the gait laboratory: synchronised capture of lower-limb kinematics, gait cycles, ground-reaction force (GRF), centre of pressure (COP) and muscle activity. I then turned the measurements into inputs the model can use, including body-segment parameters.

The results are under peer review, so they aren't reproduced here. I'm happy to discuss the method and findings directly.

R2 · Exoskeleton rehabilitation · TIET–UQ CoEOngoing since Jun 2025

R3 (Realize, Revive & Recover): AI-enabled, data-driven exoskeleton rehabilitation

A robot can move perfectly and still move a human badly. R3 develops a custom wearable lower-limb exoskeleton and evaluates it through the biomechanics of the person wearing it.

The work spans robotics, human–robot collaboration and biomechanics. I contribute to the mechanical side of exoskeleton development and run the experimental side: synchronised kinematics, GRF, COP and EMG during exoskeleton-assisted gait experiments.

Mentors: Prof. Ashish Singla, Dr. Sachin Kansal (TIET) · Dr. Alejandro Melendez-Calderon (UQ)

R3 · BiomechanicsFinal preparation · J. Biomechanics

Load-dependent changes in lower-limb joint kinetics and hip–knee coordination during walking

How does carrying load change the joint kinetics of walking, and how the hip and knee coordinate?

This is a companion analysis built on the same instrumented gait dataset as R1. The manuscript is in final preparation, so no results are reported here yet.

R4 · Planetary roboticsUnder review · IPRoMM 2026 Patent application published

Multi-axis robotic subsystem for in-situ soil sampling and analysis

Can a rover-mounted subsystem penetrate, extract, transfer and chemically characterise soil reliably enough for a timed field mission?

This is the engineering research behind the ABEx subsystems on Alaknanda and Ganga. At IRC 2026, Ganga achieved 150 mm soil penetration and 12 g sample recovery. Related IP: An Apparatus, System and Method for Robotic Soil Analysis and Collection (Application No. 202511062827).

07 About

Mechanisms first, then the intelligence inside the machine.

I'm a final-year B.E. student in Mechatronics Engineering at the Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology (TIET), Patiala. I started with mechanisms, CAD, fabrication and FEA, and kept moving closer to what makes a machine behave well: sensing, kinematics, dynamics, control and the way robots interact with people.

My goal is to build intelligent robotic systems that augment human capability, take on hard real-world problems, and work outside the lab as well as in it. I'm looking for graduate research positions in robotics and robotics R&D roles where modelling and hardware meet.
