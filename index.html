01 About

From building machines to understanding them.

Build · 2024–26

Machines that have to survive the field.

On Team MARS I designed and fabricated the soil-sampling subsystem for the rover Alaknanda. It scored the highest ABEx points at IRC 2025. As Team Captain of Ganga, I then led mechanical development across a 5-DOF manipulator, the redesigned sampling system and new PU wheels. We won IRC 2026, 1st of 33.

How Ganga was built →
Understand · 2025–now

Why machines move the way they do, and how they move people.

At the TIET–UQ Centre of Excellence I built the gait-lab workflow: motion capture, force plates and EMG, synchronised. I use it to model the human leg with Euler–Lagrange dynamics and to evaluate a wearable lower-limb exoskeleton. That work is now under review at IEEE Transactions on Robotics.

Inside the exoskeleton research →
Prove

Every idea gets built, tested and measured.

A stair-climber that went from FEA to a tracked prototype on a real step. A competition drone sized from thrust calculations. A Sim2Real digital twin of a mobile manipulator, built with MathWorks. I report what was measured, and nothing more.
