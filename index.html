01 Profile

A hands-on engineer working across mechanical design, embedded control and experimental research.

I am a final-year B.E. student in Mechatronics, Automation & Robotics Engineering at the Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology (TIET), Patiala, with a CGPA of 9.29/10 through my 6th semester. My work sits at the intersection of mechanical design, robot kinematics & motion control, biomechanics and human-motion data analysis, and manufacturing & fabrication.

On the build side, I develop physical robotic systems end-to-end — from CAD and simulation through fabrication and field testing: a multi-axis soil-sampling and analysis subsystem for planetary-analogue rovers, competition rovers and drones, a stair-climbing load carrier, and robotic-manipulator kinematic validation — designed in SolidWorks, analysed in ANSYS, and fabricated through 3D printing, CNC and conventional machining.

On the research side, I contribute to the TIET–UQ Centre of Excellence's R3 programme on exoskeleton-enabled rehabilitation and human gait analysis — motion-capture, force-plate and EMG experiments, Euler–Lagrange dynamics, and data-driven validation. I also lead Team MARS, TIET's robotics team, as General Secretary and Team Captain.

03 Research — Exoskeleton rehabilitation & gait biomechanics

Modelling how the human leg moves — and validating it against the lab.

Within the TIET–UQ Centre of Excellence's R3 programme, under Prof. Ashish Singla, I work on exoskeleton-enabled rehabilitation and human gait analysis: mechanical design and CAD of the exoskeleton, and the experimental gait-analysis pipeline behind an analytical dynamics framework.

Fig · Synchronised gait acquisition — Qualisys motion capture + AMTI force plates + Noraxon EMG → QTM → Visual3D → MATLAB.
Manuscript · Submitted

Experimental Validation of a Unified Euler–Lagrange Framework Using a Three-Link Leg Model for Human Gait Analysis

A moving-base Euler–Lagrange inverse-dynamics formulation for a planar three-link lower-limb model (thigh–shank–foot), integrating Denavit–Hartenberg kinematics with experimentally measured hip translation, ground-reaction forces and centre-of-pressure. Validated against Visual3D across five subjects and 0/8/16 kg loading using RMSE and Bland–Altman analysis.

04 Selected work

Robotic systems, designed and taken to field.

Flagship Planetary sampling

Mars Rover “Ganga” & ABEx — Multi-Axis Robotic Soil-Sampling Subsystem

International Rover Challenge 2026 · Champion · Excavation → transfer → sensing → chemical analysis, in one compact mechanism.

Led mechanical and astrobiology-expedition development of the IRC 2026 champion rover, designing the ABEx multi-axis soil-sampling subsystem: a vertical hollow-shaft NEMA-23 auger drive (with an independent IG32 rotary auger in hybrid metal + PLA-CF), a centrally-actuated rail-guided analytical tray, and a closed-loop ST3215 rotary index across six stations — funnels, reagent-delivery chemical tests, and an auger-cleaning brush.

Redesigned the horizontal sample-handling mechanism around a single lead-screw/hollow-shaft NEMA-23 stepper, eliminating dual-actuator synchronisation and tray skew; achieved 150 mm soil penetration and 12 g sample recovery across development trials.

Team contribution

Full subsystem design, integration and field deployment by Team MARS TIET across the excavation-to-analysis workflow.

My contribution

Led mechanical & astrobiology-expedition development; first author of the resulting research paper. Work spans the soil-sampling mechanism lineage across Mars Rover III & IV.
