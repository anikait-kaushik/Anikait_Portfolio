MATLAB–ROS × LoCoBot: build it in simulation, prove it on hardware

I'm developing a MATLAB–ROS framework, using MATLAB Copilot and a Simscape-based digital twin of the Trossen Robotics LoCoBot. It brings robot kinematics, dynamics, sensing and control interfaces into one Sim2Real workflow for simulation-driven development and physical validation.

01Why a digital twin

Developing behaviour directly on hardware is slow and risky. A physics-based twin lets kinematics, dynamics, sensing and control be developed and tested in simulation first. The aim is to then move to the physical robot through the same interfaces, so that what worked in simulation can be checked against reality rather than rewritten for it.
